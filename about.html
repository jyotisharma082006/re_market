ABOUT REMARKET

Good products deserve a second life.

A simple second-hand marketplace for buying and selling useful pre-owned products.

OUR IDEA

Simple, practical and easy to use.

ReMarket connects people who want to sell products they no longer need with people looking for useful products at better prices.

The website is designed as a frontend-only marketplace project with registration, login, product browsing, selling and personal listings.

03Publish your own products with image and details.
